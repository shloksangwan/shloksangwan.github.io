P.01 · Featured · Live product

MolBhav

Real Pune mandi prices and what they'll do next. MolBhav helps restaurants and cloud kitchens buy tomatoes, onions and potatoes at the right time, using daily AGMARKNET data from data.gov.in, 3-month price charts, a 14-day estimate and a simple Buy now / Wait / Steady signal.

About

I'm a first-year B.Tech CSE student at upGrad School of Technology (ADYPU Campus). I learn best by building, so most of what I know comes from shipping projects that real people can use.

With MolBhav, I helped turn a simple question ("when should a kitchen buy onions?") into a live product that reads Government mandi data and gives restaurants a clear buy, wait or steady signal.

Outside class, I'm part of the student tech community at uGSOT. I was on the team that organised an open-source session with GirlScript Foundation for 116 students.
